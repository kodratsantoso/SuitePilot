# Product Vision

## Product Name

**AI NetSuite Implementation Operating System**
(Internal codename: NetSuite AI OS)

---

## Problem Statement

NetSuite ERP implementations are among the most complex professional services engagements in the mid-market and enterprise segment. They require coordinated expertise across presales, solution architecture, functional consulting, technical development, project management, and governance — often simultaneously and under significant time pressure.

The core problems we are solving:

1. **Inconsistent delivery quality** — Output quality depends heavily on the seniority and experience of individual consultants. There is no systematic way to encode best practices into the delivery process.
2. **Slow document generation** — BRDs, fit-gap analyses, UAT scripts, integration designs, and training materials take significant manual effort to produce and are frequently inconsistent.
3. **Scope creep from poor discovery** — Inadequate presales discovery leads to underscoped projects that blow up in delivery.
4. **Knowledge loss** — Institutional knowledge about NetSuite modules, integration patterns, and implementation approaches lives in people's heads, not in structured, queryable systems.
5. **No structured governance for AI-assisted work** — As AI tools proliferate, teams use them ad-hoc without guardrails, auditability, or quality control.

---

## Strategic Positioning

This platform is **not** a generic AI assistant bolted onto a project management tool.

It is a **purpose-built operating system for NetSuite implementation delivery** that:

- Encodes the delivery methodology into structured AI agent workflows
- Enforces human review at every high-risk decision point
- Maintains full auditability of AI-assisted work
- Builds a proprietary knowledge base from real implementation experience
- Enables consistent, scalable, repeatable delivery quality regardless of team seniority

The closest analogies are:
- A delivery methodology platform (like a structured PMO framework) but AI-native
- A knowledge management system (like Confluence) but with active AI agents rather than passive documents
- An AI orchestration layer (like LangChain) but opinionated, domain-specific, and production-ready

---

## Core Capabilities (Long-Term)

1. **AI-Assisted Presales Discovery** — Structured discovery sessions with AI-generated summaries, gap identification, and module recommendations
2. **Solution Architecture Engine** — Automated fit-gap analysis, solution blueprints, and integration architecture generation
3. **Document Generation Suite** — BRDs, functional design documents, technical specs, UAT scripts, training materials
4. **PMO & Governance Automation** — Project plans, RAID logs, cutover checklists, hypercare tracking
5. **Knowledge Base & RAG Layer** — Queryable repository of NetSuite best practices, module guides, integration patterns, and implementation learnings
6. **Human Review & Approval Workflow** — Structured review gates before any AI output is used in client deliverables
7. **Hallucination Detection & Quality Scoring** — Automated checks against known NetSuite facts, flagging uncertain or contradictory AI outputs
8. **Audit Trail** — Full history of AI interactions, human decisions, and document versions

---

## Long-Term Vision

In three to five years, this platform will be the standard operating environment for NetSuite implementation firms that want to:

- Deliver consistently high-quality implementations at scale
- Onboard and ramp junior consultants faster using AI-guided workflows
- Differentiate their practice with proprietary AI-powered delivery tools
- Reduce delivery risk through structured governance and hallucination control
- Eventually offer this as a SaaS product to other NetSuite partners and implementation teams globally

The ultimate vision is a platform where a properly governed AI agent team can handle the first draft of 70–80% of implementation deliverables, with humans focused on judgment, client relationships, and final approval — rather than on repetitive document creation.

---

## Non-Goals (Phase 0)

- This is not a NetSuite connector or native SuiteApp
- This does not replace human judgment on compliance, tax, or accounting decisions
- This does not automate deployment to NetSuite instances
- This is not a general-purpose AI chatbot
